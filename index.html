HOW TO PLAY

  • Move — WASD · Jump — Space · Sprint — Shift · Crouch — Ctrl
  • Shoot — Left click · Aim / Scope — Right click · Reload — R
  • Weapons — 1 Rifle · 2 Pistol · 3 Sniper · 4 Banana · 5 Pickaxe
  • Fast build (1v1.lol-style) — Z wall · X floor · C ramp (place instantly). Q toggles build mode for the cursor. 1/2/3 selects wood/stone/metal material while in build.
  • Edit — G to enter edit mode on the structure you're aiming at. Click cells to toggle, press G again to confirm.
  • Harvest — Hold E (or use pickaxe) on trees, rocks, crates to gather materials.
  • Scoreboard — Tab · Menu — Esc
  • Multiplayer — Host gets a room code, friends paste it to join. Works peer-to-peer; no server needed.
  • Earn coins — Per kill, per match, more for wins. Spend in the Shop on crates, gun skins, charms, and bean characters.
